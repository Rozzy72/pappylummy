# PappyLummy artwork provenance

The owner authorised generated artwork to reproduce the supplied JerseyKits board while retaining PappyLummy branding. All assets used the built-in imagegen tool. None are verified merchandise photographs or evidence of stock, licensing, sponsorship or authenticity.

## Promotional assets

Saved in the original project under `public/campaigns/pappylummy-{hero,banner,club,national,retro,custom}-v2.png`.

Hero prompt: Recreate only the top-left board's wide stadium hero background, no UI or text; a football athlete in an emerald/white patterned number-10 jersey positioned right of centre, looking upward left, dark stadium negative space on the left, realistic sports photography, matching forest-green lighting and composition.

Banner prompt: Wide dark green stadium background, green patterned jersey back in the centre with YOUR NAME and 10, negative space on the left and right for live copy, matching the board's Make It Yours banner.

Category prompts: Four separate realistic portrait marketing images on dark backgrounds: red club jersey front; patterned green national-team jersey front; vintage black/white striped jersey front; blue custom jersey back with YOUR NAME and 10. Match the board's lighting and composition. No UI, category labels or arrows.

## Lettering illustrations

Saved under `public/illustrations/backs/{jersey-slug}-v1.png`. Each image is an inferred illustration using an existing local front image as a colour/cut reference, except Nigeria home which uses the supplied board as a colour reference. They must remain separate from the validated product-photo pipeline.

Common prompt: High resolution 4:5 portrait, straight-on back view, whole garment visible, neutral pale grey studio background, realistic fabric/seams, symmetrical garment, shallow back collar, no person/mannequin. Collar near 8% canvas height, hem near 94%, shoulders about 82% canvas width. Blank upper back for live lettering: no names, numbers, words, logos or centre-back prints. Match the supplied front's colours and trim; the result is a generated lettering illustration, not verified merchandise photography.

Variants: Arsenal red/white sleeves; Barcelona red/blue stripes with yellow trim; Bayern red/white trim; Brazil yellow/green trim; Chelsea off-white/blue trim; Liverpool deep red/pinstripes; Manchester City sky-blue/navy trim; Manchester United red/black trim; Nigeria retro geometric green/white; Real Madrid white/dark piping; Nigeria home emerald geometric green/white trim.

Names and numbers are rendered separately as live SVG text, so all selected names/numbers remain editable and the artwork cannot fix Nigeria or a printed number across other jerseys.
